# Portfolio / Résumé Wording

## One-line summary

**OpsPilot — AI-assisted infrastructure operations platform integrating Zabbix, VMware vCenter, active diagnostics, incident correlation, safe remediation workflows, verification, and bilingual operator assistance.**

## Résumé bullets

- Built OpsPilot, a multi-site infrastructure operations platform integrating Zabbix, VMware vCenter, and active ICMP/TCP diagnostics.
- Implemented incident correlation and root-cause classification to turn fragmented monitoring signals into operator-oriented incident context.
- Designed playbook and remediation workflows with human approval for sensitive infrastructure changes.
- Added post-action verification and audit history to validate recovery instead of assuming command success.
- Built bilingual Persian/English Telegram operator workflows and a web dashboard for operational visibility.
- Added an AI operator layer that uses grounded infrastructure context and fails safely when the provider is unavailable or rate-limited.

## Interview talking points

- Why OpsPilot complements rather than replaces Zabbix/vCenter.
- Why correlation is different from simple alert aggregation.
- Why human approval is important for infrastructure-changing actions.
- Why verification must be independent from command execution.
- How deterministic operational evidence and AI assistance should be combined safely.
